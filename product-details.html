If you live with anxious attachment, you may know the exhausting rhythm: you feel close, then something shifts - a slower reply, a shorter message, a change of plans - and your mind races to fill in the gaps. You reach for certainty, often through reassurance seeking, but the comfort fades fast and the urge returns stronger. The problem is not that you care too much. It is that your nervous system has learnt that closeness equals safety, and uncertainty equals danger.

Attachment, Not Anxiety is a practical guide to building security without constant reassurance. Saira Nazmeen-Grey shows you how anxious patterns are triggered, how the reassurance loop keeps you stuck, and how to replace urgency with steadier emotional regulation. You will learn self-soothing skills that work in the moment, communication skills that turn hints and tests into clear requests, and relationship boundaries that support connection rather than control.
